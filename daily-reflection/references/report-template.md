# Report Template

Use only sections that add value, but always include Scope and omissions, Applied
local changes, and Durable records.

## Scope and omissions

- Period and timezone
- Projects/CWD labels and task IDs reviewed
- Interactive, programmatic App Server, subagent, and archived inclusion choices
- Collector limits, transient complete-object boundary, unreadable tasks, privacy
  omissions, and unavailable evidence

## Day summary

Describe the work and highest-value lesson in two to five sentences. Separate facts
from inference.

## Wins

For each reinforced behavior:

- **What worked**
- **Evidence**
- **Why preserve it**

## Corrections and friction

For each material issue:

- **Observation**
- **Evidence**
- **Cause or uncertainty**
- **Impact**
- **Confidence**

## Open loops

List unfinished work, unresolved decisions, deferred risks, and promised follow-ups.
Do not present a recommendation as an accepted commitment.

## Reusable lessons

For each lesson:

- **Lesson**
- **Scope**: task, project, skill, or user-wide
- **Evidence strength**: High, Medium, or Low
- **Limits/conflicts**

## Applied local changes

For each automatic change:

- **Target**
- **Evidence and confidence**
- **Why the automatic gate passed**
- **Exact change**
- **Validation**
- **Rollback status or method**

If no automatic change qualified, say so plainly.

## Recorded observations

Summarize findings retained without changing behavior and why they did not qualify.

## Changes requiring approval

For each gated change:

- **Target**
- **Exact proposed change**
- **Evidence and confidence**
- **Triggered high-impact condition**
- **Benefit**
- **Risk/privacy impact**
- **Verification and rollback plan**
- **Specific decision requested**

Only changes listed under Applied local changes were made. No approval-required change
was applied. Ask only for the specific gated decisions that remain.

## Durable records

- Reflection path, or why no report was saved
- Friction entries appended
- State advancement and project checkpoint status
- Whether the reflection is available to a future weekly-report workflow
